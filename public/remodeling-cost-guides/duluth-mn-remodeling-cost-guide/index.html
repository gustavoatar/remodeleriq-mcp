Things to know in MN

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: Minnesota requires contractor licensing through DLI Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry licenses contractors—verify at dli.mn.gov. Residential Building Contractor and Residential Remodeler licenses are different classifications.

Regional insight

Extreme Cold Construction: Minnesota winters regularly reach -20°F or colder The extreme cold demands exceptional insulation and air sealing. Reddit emphasizes R-60+ attic insulation, triple-pane windows in severe areas, and quality furnaces.

Ice Dams & Winter Roof Issues: Minnesota Ice dams are a major issue.

Reddit strongly emphasizes proper attic insulation and ventilation as the solution—not heated cables. Ice/water shield should extend well past eaves.

Regional insight

Radon Testing: Minnesota has significant radon levels statewide Minnesota has high radon levels throughout. Reddit strongly recommends testing before basement finishing. Radon-resistant new construction is now code.

Regional insight

Basement Moisture & Waterproofing: Spring snowmelt creates serious basement water issues The rapid spring thaw causes widespread basement flooding. Reddit recommends robust sump pump systems with battery backup. Exterior drainage improvements help.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
