State-Specific Risks

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: Alabama requires licensing through the Home Builders Licensure Board Alabama Home Builders Licensure Board licenses residential contractors—verify at hblb.alabama.gov. License is required for projects over $10,000.

Regional insight

Tornado Preparedness: Alabama is in Tornado Alley with frequent severe storms Alabama sees significant tornado activity, especially in the northern half. Reddit recommends safe rooms (FEMA-rated), wind-rated garage doors, and proper roof tie-downs.

Regional insight

Hurricane Risk (Gulf Coast): Mobile and the Gulf Coast face hurricane impacts Coastal Alabama sees regular hurricane impacts. Reddit emphasizes hurricane straps, impact-resistant windows or shutters, and flood zone awareness.

Regional insight

Humidity & Moisture Control: Alabama High humidity causes mold and moisture issues. Reddit emphasizes proper HVAC sizing for dehumidification, crawl space encapsulation, and moisture-resistant materials.

Regional insight

Termite Prevention: Alabama has severe termite pressure Subterranean termites are prevalent throughout Alabama. Reddit strongly recommends pre-treatment, regular inspections, and maintaining termite bonds.

Five questions Birmingham homeowners should ask

  1. Is your license active in AL? Verify with the state licensing board before work starts.
  2. What does the warranty cover? Clarify labor vs manufacturer coverage and duration.
  3. How are change orders priced? Get a written process and markup rules.
  4. What is the payment schedule? Avoid large upfront cash; tie payments to milestones.
  5. Are permits and fees included? For Addition in Birmingham, confirm AHJ fees and plans are in scope or called out.
