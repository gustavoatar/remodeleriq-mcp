Things to know in NC

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: North Carolina requires licensing for contractors on projects over $30,000 NC Licensing Board issues General Contractor licenses (verify at nclbgc.org). Projects under $30,000 don

Regional insight

Hurricane Preparedness (Coastal): Coastal NC is vulnerable to hurricanes with specific building code requirements Coastal counties have stricter wind-load requirements. Reddit users recommend hurricane straps, impact-resistant windows or shutters, and proper roof tie-downs. Wind mitigation can reduce insurance costs.

Regional insight

Moisture & Humidity Control: NC Crawl spaces are common and prone to moisture issues. Reddit strongly recommends encapsulated crawl spaces over vented ones. Dehumidification is often needed in basements and crawl spaces.

Regional insight

Crawl Space Encapsulation: Traditional vented crawl spaces don NC building code now allows (and many recommend) sealed, conditioned crawl spaces. Reddit users report significant comfort and energy improvements after encapsulation. Typical cost is $5,000-15,000.

Regional insight

Termites & Wood-Destroying Insects: NC has significant termite pressure, especially in coastal and piedmont areas Both subterranean and drywood termites are present. Reddit users recommend termite bonds with annual inspections, treated lumber for any ground contact, and inspection before major renovations.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
