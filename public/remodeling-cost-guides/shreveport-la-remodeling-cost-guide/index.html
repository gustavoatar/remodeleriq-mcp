Things to know in LA

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: Louisiana requires licensing through the State Licensing Board Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors licenses residential work over $7,500—verify at lslbc.louisiana.gov. Different classifications exist for different work types.

Regional insight

Hurricane Preparedness: Louisiana faces significant hurricane risk throughout the coast The entire Gulf Coast sees hurricane impacts. Reddit emphasizes hurricane straps, impact windows or shutters, wind-rated roofing, and elevation where required.

Regional insight

Flood Zones & Elevation: Much of Louisiana is in FEMA flood zones Flood zones affect most of coastal Louisiana. Substantial improvement rules may require elevation. Reddit strongly recommends checking flood zone status before major work.

Regional insight

Extreme Humidity & Moisture: Louisiana has some of the highest humidity in the US Humidity is relentless. Reddit emphasizes dehumidification, proper HVAC sizing, and moisture-resistant materials throughout. Mold prevention is critical.

Regional insight

High Water Table: Louisiana The high water table makes basements impossible and affects slab construction. Reddit notes that elevated construction is common for flood protection.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
