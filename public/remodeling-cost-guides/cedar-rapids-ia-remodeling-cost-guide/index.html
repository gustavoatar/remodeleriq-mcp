Things to know in IA

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

No Statewide Contractor License: Iowa has no statewide general contractor licensing Iowa doesn

Regional insight

Radon Testing: Iowa has some of the highest radon levels in the country Iowa consistently ranks among the highest states for radon. Reddit very strongly recommends testing before any basement work. Radon-resistant new construction is recommended.

Regional insight

Severe Weather & Tornadoes: Iowa experiences tornadoes and severe storms Iowa sees regular severe storms and tornadoes. Reddit recommends safe rooms, wind-rated materials, and proper roof tie-downs.

Regional insight

Extreme Temperature Swings: Iowa temperatures range from -20°F to 100°F+ The extreme temperature range stresses materials and HVAC systems. Reddit emphasizes proper insulation, air sealing, and quality equipment.

Regional insight

Basement Moisture: Iowa basements commonly have moisture issues Wet basements are common. Reddit recommends addressing exterior drainage and proper sump pump systems with battery backup.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
