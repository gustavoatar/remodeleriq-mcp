Do you need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Fort Collins?

Yes, when fixtures move or new wiring/venting goes in. Swapping a vanity or retiling in place is usually exempt in Fort Collins. Permit fees for a typical $22,684 bathroom remodel run about $125–$500 (valuation-based fee plus plan review), and CO plan review typically takes 2–4 weeks.

Bathroom Remodel permits in Fort Collins: FAQ

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Fort Collins?
Yes, when fixtures move or new wiring/venting goes in. Swapping a vanity or retiling in place is usually exempt in Fort Collins, CO. When in doubt, call the Fort Collins building department before work starts — unpermitted work can block a future sale.
How much do bathroom remodel permits cost in Fort Collins?
Expect roughly $125–$500 in Fort Collins for a typical $22,684 bathroom remodel. Most jurisdictions charge a valuation-based fee of about 0.5–2% of project value, plus plan review and per-trade permit minimums.
How long does a bathroom remodel permit take in Fort Collins?
Simple trade permits are often issued over the counter in Fort Collins. Where plan review applies, CO turnaround typically runs 2–4 weeks. Submit complete drawings the first time — resubmittals are the most common cause of delay.
Who pulls the permit — me or my contractor?
Usually your contractor pulls permits for a bathroom remodel, and their license is attached to the work. A contractor who asks you to pull an owner's permit for contractor-performed work is shifting liability onto you — treat that as a red flag when reviewing bids.
