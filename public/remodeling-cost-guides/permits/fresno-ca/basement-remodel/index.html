Basement Remodel permits in Fresno: FAQ

Do I need a permit for a basement remodel in Fresno?
Yes — finishing a basement almost always needs building, electrical, and often plumbing permits, plus egress compliance for any bedroom in Fresno, CA. When in doubt, call the Fresno building department before work starts — unpermitted work can block a future sale.
How much do basement remodel permits cost in Fresno?
Expect roughly $250–$1,000 in Fresno for a typical $50,324 basement remodel. Most jurisdictions charge a valuation-based fee of about 0.5–2% of project value, plus plan review and per-trade permit minimums.
How long does a basement remodel permit take in Fresno?
Simple trade permits are often issued over the counter in Fresno. Where plan review applies, CA turnaround typically runs 4–10 weeks. Submit complete drawings the first time — resubmittals are the most common cause of delay.
Who pulls the permit — me or my contractor?
Usually your contractor pulls permits for a basement remodel, and their license is attached to the work. A contractor who asks you to pull an owner's permit for contractor-performed work is shifting liability onto you — treat that as a red flag when reviewing bids.
