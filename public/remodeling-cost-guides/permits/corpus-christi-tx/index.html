Corpus Christi remodeling permits: FAQ

How much does a remodeling permit cost in Corpus Christi?
In Corpus Christi, building permit fees typically run $375–$1,525 for a mid-size project — most jurisdictions charge a valuation-based fee of roughly 0.5–2% of project cost plus plan review. For a typical $76,248 kitchen remodel, budget toward the middle of that range.
Do I need a permit to remodel in Corpus Christi, TX?
Structural work, electrical, plumbing, roofing, decks, and additions require permits in Corpus Christi. Purely cosmetic work — painting, flooring, cabinet refacing — generally does not. When in doubt, call the Corpus Christi building department before work starts; unpermitted work can block a future home sale.
How long does a Corpus Christi remodeling permit take?
Simple trade permits (electrical, mechanical) are often same-day or a few days over-the-counter in Corpus Christi. Projects needing plan review — additions, ADUs, structural changes — typically take 2–6 weeks, longer in high-volume metros. Build the wait into your project timeline.
What happens if I remodel without a permit in Corpus Christi?
Unpermitted work in TX can mean stop-work orders, fines, forced removal, and problems at resale (buyers and appraisers flag it). A contractor who suggests skipping permits to save time is a red flag worth scrutinizing.
