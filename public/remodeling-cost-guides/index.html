Regional variance

A kitchen gut in NYC can run around $300/sq ft, while Houston might average closer to $100/sq ft—markets differ. Each guide scales benchmarks to that metro market, not a one-size-fits-all national number.

The maintenance tax

The average homeowner now pays thousands yearly in hidden, unforeseen costs, and over 20% put off major upgrades due to not finding a trustworthy contractor. Helping you not get scammed when modernizing your home and finding a trustworthy contractor is why RemodelerIQ was built.
