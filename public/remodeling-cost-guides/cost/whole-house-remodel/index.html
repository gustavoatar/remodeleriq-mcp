Where the money goes: labor vs. materials

Across the 152 metros tracked, labor averages about 50% of a whole house remodel budget and materials about 50%. When you review a bid, judge its labor line against your metro's trade wages — the biggest source of overpricing we see — not a national average.

How we calculate these numbers

Every figure on this page is a blend of three independent cost sources — construction cost data (70%), homeowner-reported project spend (15%), and live U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics trade wages (15%) — localized to each metro with a regional cost factor and a current-year escalation index. Ranges refresh as the underlying wage and materials data moves, which is why they're dated 2026 rather than recycled from older surveys.

Whole House Remodel costs: FAQ

How much does a whole house remodel cost in 2026?
A whole house remodel typically costs $60,760 to $285,930 in 2026, with a national median around $124,538. Actual pricing varies by metro — San Francisco runs highest and McAllen lowest among the 152 markets tracked here.
Which cities have the highest whole house remodel costs?
Among the 152 metros tracked, San Francisco, San Jose, New York carry the highest whole house remodel costs in 2026, driven by local labor wages and material logistics. McAllen sits at the lower end.
Why does whole house remodel cost vary so much by city?
The same whole house remodel scope is priced differently across metros because of local trade wages, permit timelines, and material delivery costs. RemodelerIQ localizes every estimate using live 2026 BLS wage data for each metro rather than a single national average.
How do I know if my whole house remodel bid is fair?
Find your city below for the local 2026 range, then compare your contractor's bid against it. RemodelerIQ analyzes your actual PDF estimate against live local labor and material data for free, flagging padded scope and inflated margins.
How much of a whole house remodel cost is labor vs. materials?
Across the 152 metros tracked, labor averages about 50% of a whole house remodel budget and materials about 50%. The split shifts with finish level and local wages — which is why a bid's labor line should be judged against local rates, not national ones.
Do I need a permit for a whole house remodel?
Most whole house remodel projects that touch structure, electrical, or plumbing require permits; purely cosmetic scopes usually don't. Permit fees typically run 0.5–2% of project value. See our city-by-city building permit guides for local fees, rules, and timelines.
