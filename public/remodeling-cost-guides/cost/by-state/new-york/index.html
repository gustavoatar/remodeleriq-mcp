New York remodeling watch-outs

  • ›NYC DOB Permits & Complexity: New York City has one of the most complex permitting systems in the country NYC requires permits for almost everything, and the DOB process can take months. Reddit users strongly recommend using contractors experienced with NYC DOB filings. Self-certified filings are faster but shift liability to the homeowner if issues arise.
  • ›Landmark & Historic District Rules: Many NYC and Upstate areas have landmark restrictions that limit renovation options Work visible from the street in landmark districts requires LPC approval before DOB permits. Reddit users report approval taking 3-6+ months. Even interior work in landmarked buildings may have restrictions.
  • ›Coop & Condo Board Approvals: NYC coop and condo renovations require board approval, which can delay projects significantly Boards may require alteration agreements, architect review, insurance certificates, and deposits. Reddit users recommend starting the approval process 2-3 months before planned construction. Some buildings have blackout periods.
  • ›High Labor Costs: New York has some of the highest construction labor costs in the US NYC labor costs are 50-100% above national average. Upstate is more reasonable but still above average. Reddit warns that lowball bids often mean unlicensed workers or shortcuts. Get multiple bids but be suspicious of prices far below others.

Which New York city is most expensive to remodel in?
New York carries the highest remodeling costs among New York metros tracked here, while Buffalo is the most affordable. The gap is driven by local labor wages, permit timelines, and material logistics.
Do I need a permit to remodel in New York?
Most structural, electrical, and plumbing work in New York requires a permit, and requirements vary by city and county. Permit-exempt cosmetic work (paint, flooring, cabinet refacing) usually does not. Check your specific city guide below for local rules.
