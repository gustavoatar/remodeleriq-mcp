South Carolina remodeling watch-outs

  • ›State Contractor Licensing: South Carolina requires contractor licensing through LLR South Carolina LLR licenses residential contractors—verify at llr.sc.gov. General Contractor and Residential Builder are different classifications.
  • ›Hurricane Preparedness (Coastal): Coastal South Carolina faces serious hurricane risk Charleston, Myrtle Beach, and coastal areas see regular hurricane impacts. Reddit emphasizes hurricane straps, impact-resistant windows or shutters, and proper roof tie-downs.
  • ›Humidity & Moisture Control: South Carolina High humidity causes mold and moisture issues. Reddit emphasizes proper HVAC sizing for dehumidification, crawl space encapsulation, and moisture-resistant materials.
  • ›Termite Prevention: South Carolina has severe termite pressure Subterranean termites are prevalent throughout SC. Reddit recommends pre-treatment for new construction, regular inspections, and maintaining termite bonds.

South Carolina remodeling costs: FAQ

How much does remodeling cost in South Carolina?
Across South Carolina's 4 tracked metros, a kitchen remodel runs $29,228 to $164,357 in 2026 (median $79,214); other projects scale similarly. Costs vary by metro — pick your city below for localized 2026 pricing.
Which South Carolina city is most expensive to remodel in?
Charleston carries the highest remodeling costs among South Carolina metros tracked here, while Columbia is the most affordable. The gap is driven by local labor wages, permit timelines, and material logistics.
Do I need a permit to remodel in South Carolina?
Most structural, electrical, and plumbing work in South Carolina requires a permit, and requirements vary by city and county. Permit-exempt cosmetic work (paint, flooring, cabinet refacing) usually does not. Check your specific city guide below for local rules.
