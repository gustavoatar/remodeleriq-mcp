Things to know in TN

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: Tennessee requires licensing for contractors on projects over $25,000 Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors requires licensing for projects exceeding $25,000—verify at tn.gov/commerce. Below that threshold, no state license is required but local rules may apply.

Regional insight

Humidity & Moisture Control: Tennessee High humidity from May through September causes mold and moisture issues. Reddit emphasizes proper ventilation, dehumidification in crawl spaces and basements, and moisture-resistant materials.

Regional insight

Termites & Pest Pressure: Tennessee has significant termite and pest pressure Subterranean termites are prevalent throughout Tennessee. Reddit recommends pre-treatment for new construction, regular inspections, and maintaining termite bonds on existing homes.

Regional insight

Nashville/Memphis Growth Areas: Rapid growth has led to construction quality concerns Nashville and Memphis suburbs have seen rapid development. Reddit warns about rushed construction and inexperienced crews. Inspect new construction carefully before closing.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
