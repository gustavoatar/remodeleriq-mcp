Things to know in NM

From real homeowner and contractor discussions in your state.

Regional insight

State Contractor Licensing: New Mexico requires contractor licensing through CID New Mexico Construction Industries Division (CID) licenses contractors—verify at rld.nm.gov. Different classifications exist for different work types.

Regional insight

Adobe & Stucco Construction: New Mexico has unique adobe and stucco building traditions Adobe and stucco require specific maintenance and repair techniques. Reddit recommends contractors experienced with traditional New Mexico construction.

Regional insight

Extreme UV & Sun Exposure: High altitude means intense UV exposure UV degrades materials faster at altitude. Reddit recommends UV-resistant materials, quality sealants, and understanding accelerated wear.

Regional insight

Water Scarcity & Conservation: New Mexico has severe water restrictions Water is extremely limited. Xeriscaping is common or required. Reddit notes that rainwater harvesting is encouraged.

Regional insight

High Altitude Construction: Much of New Mexico is at high altitude Santa Fe and mountain areas need altitude-adjusted appliances. Concrete curing is affected. Reddit recommends experienced contractors.

Pro Tip: Don't ignore the "lock-in" effect.

With many homeowners improving in place, local contractors are in high demand. If a bid looks too good to be true, get it reviewed before you sign—inspectors may catch code or quality issues later that cost you more.
