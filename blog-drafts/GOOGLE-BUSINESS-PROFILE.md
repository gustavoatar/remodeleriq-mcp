## Second post (publish in 1 week)

**Title:** The 3 Lines Where Kitchen Quotes Hide Thousands

Most kitchen remodel bids look reasonable at the total — but the line items tell a different story.

Three places to scan before you sign:
1. Cabinet install labor should be 15–22% of cabinet material cost. Not 35%+.
2. General conditions / project supervision should be 5–8%. Not 12%+.
3. Allowances should name a brand AND a quantity. If they don't, you're paying the overage plus the contractor's markup.

We built a tool that flags these automatically at RemodelerIQ.com.

## Third post (publish in 2 weeks)

**Title:** Red Flag: 50% Deposit Means Trouble

If your contractor asks for 50% upfront before any work happens, walk away.

Industry standard for residential remodels is 10–15% deposit at signing, with the rest tied to milestones (material delivery, rough-in, drywall close-up, substantial completion, punch list).

When a contractor needs 50% upfront, it's almost always cash flow — they're using your money to pay for their last job. That's their financial problem, not yours.

Counter with: "10% at signing, 20% on material delivery, balance tied to milestones." If they walk, you saved yourself.

More renovation protection at RemodelerIQ.com.

## FAQ section (10 Q&As)

**1. Is RemodelerIQ free?**
Your first 3 bid analyses are completely free with no signup required. After that, premium plans start at $19.99/month for unlimited analyses.

**2. What data does RemodelerIQ use?**
We use Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics for trade labor rates, FRED Producer Price Indices for material cost trends, and Zonda's Cost vs. Value Report benchmarks — all 2026 data, updated regularly.

**3. How long does an analysis take?**
Under 90 seconds from PDF upload to results. The analysis runs in real-time using your zip code's specific labor rates and current material indices.

**4. Do I need to create an account?**
No. Your first 3 analyses run as a guest with no signup or login required.

**5. What contractor bids can RemodelerIQ analyze?**
Any PDF or photo of a contractor's written quote — kitchen, bathroom, basement, deck, roof, addition, full home renovation, etc. We support most major project types.

**6. Can RemodelerIQ verify if my contractor is licensed?**
Yes — our Trusted Radar tool checks state licensing boards and consumer protection databases to verify a contractor's active license, complaint history, and BBB rating.

**7. What metros does RemodelerIQ cover?**
National coverage with regional cost adjustments for 50+ major US metros including Atlanta, Phoenix, Dallas, Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, Seattle, Boston, and more.

**8. Will RemodelerIQ recommend a specific contractor?**
No — we don't refer or endorse specific contractors. Our role is to help you analyze the bid you've received and verify the contractor's credentials independently.

**9. Is my bid information private?**
Yes. We don't sell or share bid data with contractors or third parties. Your uploads are private to your account.

**10. Who built RemodelerIQ?**
Gustavo Atar, an Atlanta-area homeowner who got frustrated by how much contractor quotes varied for the same scope and built a spreadsheet to check them. The spreadsheet turned into the tool. Built for homeowners, by a homeowner.
